How To Choose The Right Delivery Company For Your Business

The ultimate checklist for selecting a logistics partner that scales with you.

Your delivery partner can make or break your e-commerce business. Choose the right one, and your customers rave about the experience. Choose wrong, and you'll hemorrhage money on returns, late shipments, and frustrated buyers who never come back.

Yet most merchants select a delivery company based on a single factor — price — without considering the full picture. This guide gives you the complete checklist for evaluating a logistics partner, so you make the right call the first time.

01 — Coverage Area: Can They Actually Deliver Everywhere You Need?

The first and most fundamental question: does the delivery company serve the areas where your customers live? Many providers claim "nationwide coverage" but have glaring blind spots in rural or semi-urban regions.

  • Map Their Network: Ask for a detailed coverage map. Don't settle for vague promises — get specific regions, districts, and delivery zones.
  • Test the Margins: If you have customers in Mtwara, Kigoma, or smaller towns, confirm that those areas are actively served — not technically on the map but practically unreliable.
  • Urban vs. Rural SLAs: Does the provider offer different delivery timelines for urban and rural areas? Understanding this helps you set accurate customer expectations.
  • Expansion Plans: If you plan to grow into new regions, does the provider have coverage there already or planned for the near future?

A delivery partner that can't reach your customers is not a partner at all.

02 — COD Infrastructure: How Fast Do You Get Your Money?

In Tanzania, over 80% of e-commerce transactions are Cash on Delivery. This makes COD infrastructure the single most important feature of your logistics partner. A provider that collects cash but takes weeks to deposit it is holding your working capital hostage.

  • Settlement Speed: Look for providers that settle within 24-48 hours. Anything beyond a week is unacceptable for a growing business.
  • Digital Cash Logging: Does the provider log cash collections digitally at the doorstep, or do they rely on manual paper trails? Digital logging means zero leakage.
  • Mobile Money Integration: Can customers pay via M-Pesa or Airtel Money at the door? This reduces cash handling and increases security.
  • Settlement Flexibility: Can you choose daily, weekly, or custom settlement cycles? Your cash flow needs may change as you scale.

"We switched providers because our old one took 21 days to settle COD. With ChapShop, we get paid in 48 hours. The difference in cash flow is night and day."

03 — Tracking & Visibility: Can You See Everything in Real-Time?

Blind logistics is dead. If your delivery company can't tell you exactly where a package is at any given moment, you're operating in the dark age. Real-time tracking is no longer a luxury — it's a baseline requirement.

  • Live GPS Tracking: You and your customers should be able to see the delivery rider on a live map as the package moves.
  • Automated Notifications: Status updates should be pushed automatically via SMS at every milestone — dispatched, in transit, approaching, delivered.
  • Proof of Delivery: Digital signatures, delivery photos, and timestamps protect you from disputes and chargebacks.
  • Merchant Dashboard: A single screen where you can see all your shipments, filter by status, and take action on exceptions.

Without real-time visibility, you can't proactively manage problems — you can only react to complaints.

04 — Pricing Transparency: Are There Hidden Costs?

The cheapest base rate is rarely the cheapest overall. Many providers advertise low per-delivery prices but stack on hidden fees that inflate your actual costs by 20-40%.

  • All-Inclusive Pricing: Ask for the total per-delivery cost including fuel surcharges, remote area fees, and handling charges. No surprises.
  • Return Fees: What happens when a delivery fails? Do you pay the full delivery rate again, or is the return handled at a reduced rate — or even free?
  • Volume Discounts: Does the provider offer tiered pricing that rewards your growth? As you ship more, your cost per delivery should decrease.
  • Clear Invoicing: Every charge should be itemized and accessible. If you can't understand your invoice, something is wrong.

Demand total pricing transparency upfront. A trustworthy partner has nothing to hide.

05 — Technology & Integration: Does It Fit Your Workflow?

Your delivery company shouldn't force you to change how you work. It should fit seamlessly into your existing tools and processes. Evaluate the technology stack before you commit:

  • E-Commerce Integrations: Does the provider offer plugins for Shopify, WooCommerce, or whatever platform you use? One-click integration saves weeks of setup.
  • API Quality: If you need custom integration, is the API well-documented, consistent, and quick to implement?
  • Mobile-First Dashboard: Can you manage shipments from your phone? If you're always on the move, a desktop-only platform is a dealbreaker.
  • Bulk Operations: Can you upload 100 orders at once via CSV, or do you have to enter them one by one?

The right technology doesn't add work — it removes it.

06 — Scalability: Will They Grow With You?

The delivery company that works for 10 orders a day may collapse under 500. Before you commit, ask yourself: can this partner handle 10x my current volume?

  • Service Breadth: Do they offer only standard courier, or also warehousing, fulfillment, and specialized shipping (medical, cold-chain)? You may not need these today, but you might tomorrow.
  • Unified Platform: As you add services, will you need separate accounts and dashboards? A unified platform saves enormous operational overhead.
  • Support Capacity: Does the provider have enough riders and infrastructure to absorb your growth without service degradation?
  • Account Management: As you scale, will you get a dedicated account manager who understands your business?

Switching logistics providers is painful and disruptive. Choose one that can grow with you from day one to dominance.

The Checklist Summary

Before you sign with any delivery company, run through this checklist:

✅ Full coverage in all your customer regions

✅ Fast COD settlement (24-48 hours) with digital logging

✅ Real-time GPS tracking with automated notifications

✅ All-inclusive, transparent pricing with no hidden fees

✅ Seamless technology integration with your existing tools

✅ Scalable services that grow with your business
