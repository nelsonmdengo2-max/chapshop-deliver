💡 Tip: always keep the QR on a white background and don't print it too small — minimum 2×2 cm for reliable scanning.

⚠️ Important — your link must be LIVE on the internet

A QR code can only point to a public web link (like https://afriroute.live). A file sitting on your computer will not open on someone else's phone — you must put your website online first.

🚀 How to put AFRiROUTE online for FREE (2 minutes)

  1. Netlify Drop (easiest): go to app.netlify.com/drop → drag your afriroute-dashboard.html file in → you instantly get a live link like https://afriroute-demo.netlify.app. Paste that link above and generate your QR.
  2. GitHub Pages (permanent & free): create a free account at github.com → new repository → upload your HTML file → Settings → Pages → turn it on.
  3. Vercel: vercel.com → import your file → free live link in seconds.
  4. Custom domain (optional, later): buy afriroute.live (~$10/year) and connect it — your QR stays the same as long as the domain doesn't change.
